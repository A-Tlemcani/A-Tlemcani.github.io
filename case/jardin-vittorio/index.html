Jardin Vittorio

Two private apartments in Marrakech's Hivernage, booked mostly through Airbnb and Booking.com. The owner wanted his own site to feel luxurious, simple and very clear (the previous one “felt cheap”), take bookings directly, and never double-book.

From offer to build

  • Research first: channel-manager architecture (single source of truth, signed webhooks, booking state machine), Moroccan constraints, and payments for a non-resident owner.
  • A clear offer: three fixed-price packs (capture requests → take bookings → full service with quarterly reporting) and a “what we need from you” list per pack.
  • Pre-launch checklist: money traps, 20 security items, legal and trust for Morocco and GDPR, quality.

3D without a scan

The owner had photos, not a 3D scan. So each hero photo is AI-upscaled (tiled, up to 2880 px), then split into near / middle / far layers with a depth model, with the gaps behind each layer filled by LaMa inpainting. On arrival the camera drifts and near things slide against far ones: real parallax, nothing is scaled. The cost is a few kilobytes of JS, with no 3D engine.

Engineering

  • Strict CSP (default-src 'self', no inline scripts or styles), so the site carries no third-party trackers.
  • Budgets enforced by end-to-end tests: 20 KB of JS and 25 KB of CSS per page.
  • i18n-ready (English first, Italian next), Playwright e2e suite, Conventional Commits and a PR-only workflow.
  • Accounts, domain and payments opened in the client's name; card payments only through a hosted page.
