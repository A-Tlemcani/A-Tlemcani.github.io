Dar Nila

Short-term-rental owners in Marrakech only have photos. Dar Nila shows them what their own direct-booking site becomes with a five-minute phone scan of their place: guests glide through the real courtyard in 3D, pick a room, and book.

Role
Concept, design, 3D, front-end
Stack
Astro · Three.js · Spark (Gaussian splats)
3D
Real scan (CC-BY), depth-map 2.5D
Status
Design gate: 3 directions

The problem

Owners pay 15–20% commission to platforms, and their own sites look like every other listing: a photo carousel. A guest can't feel a riad from photos. The pitch needed to answer one question in two seconds: “what does my place gain from 3D?”

What I built

  • Scroll glide through a real scan: a Gaussian-splat capture of a courtyard rendered with Spark on Three.js. A regular photo paints first and the scan streams in after.
  • “Photos only ↔ With a 3D scan” toggle: the same page tells the same story both ways, so an owner sees the difference on their own phone.
  • Three 3D moments: the glide, a cut-away dollhouse room picker, and free exploration with hotspots that open the matching room.
  • Booking flow: availability calendar, nightly price breakdown with cleaning fee and tourist tax, guest details, request-to-book confirmation (mock, no payment).
  • Honest 3D: real scans are labelled as such, AI depth effects as “AI 3D impression”, and every asset licence is credited.

Engineering

  • 3D loads after first paint and only when visible, with static fallbacks for no-WebGL2, low-power devices and reduced motion.
  • Depth maps generated offline with Depth Anything V2 Small for the 2.5D photo mode; dev-only tools never ship.
  • Frame-by-frame capture tooling to record 3D moments on a GPU-less cloud machine, plus a packer that turns each direction into one double-clickable HTML file.
  • Self-hosted fonts and assets, no CDN scripts; Conventional Commits and PR-based workflow.

For your place

A phone or LiDAR scan of your courtyard, rooms and terrace takes one visit. From it I build the same experience with your real place and no invented rooms, wired to your channel manager so the calendar never double-books.
